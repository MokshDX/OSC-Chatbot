Access Control Standard

Owner: Information Security. Review cycle: annual.

Principle of least privilege

Every account is granted the minimum access required to perform its role. Access is granted to groups, never to individual accounts, so that entitlements can be reasoned about and revoked as a unit.

Access reviews

Access to systems classified Confidential or higher is reviewed quarterly. Access to systems classified Internal is reviewed annually. Reviews are performed by the system owner, not by the security team, because the system owner is the only party who knows whether an entitlement is still justified.

An entitlement that a reviewer cannot justify is revoked by default. Reviews must complete within 15 working days of being raised.

Joiners, movers and leavers

  • Access for a new joiner is provisioned no earlier than their start date.
  • When an employee changes role, their previous entitlements are revoked within 5 working days. Additive-only role changes are the most common source of excessive privilege at OSC.
  • All access is revoked within 4 hours of an employee's departure being confirmed, and immediately for an involuntary departure.

Privileged access

Production administrative access requires hardware multi-factor authentication and is granted just-in-time for a maximum of 8 hours. Standing production administrative access is not permitted for any role.

Service accounts

Every service account has a named human owner. Credentials rotate every 90 days. Service accounts must not be used interactively, and interactive use is alerted on.
