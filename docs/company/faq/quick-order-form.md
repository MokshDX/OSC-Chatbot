# Quick Order Form

*Advance FAQ*

## Can I customize the appearance of the Quick Order Form?

Yes. The store admin can customize colors, borders, and other visual elements to match
your store's theme.

## Can I bulk upload SKUs or product lists?

At the moment, you can manually enter SKUs one by one. For bulk upload or CSV import
functionality, please connect with us at apps@oscprofessionals.com.

## Does the form support product variants?

Yes. Entering the SKU of a specific variant will ensure that exact variant is added to the
cart.

## What happens if I enter an invalid SKU?

The product will not show if the SKU is invalid, you have to enter the correct SKU.

## Is tiered pricing displayed on the Quick Order Form?

Yes, if a product variant has tiered pricing rules configured, those rules will also be
displayed in the Quick Order Form. This helps customers see quantity-based pricing
directly while placing their orders.
