# Tax Display

*Advance FAQ*

## What does the Tax Display feature do?

It allows merchants to show both inclusive and exclusive tax prices on the pricing grid,
based on the customer's selected country.

## Where can I enable the Tax Display feature?

You can enable the feature from the Settings page of the app by checking the "Enable Tax
Display" option.

## Can I set different tax rates for different countries?

Yes, you can define country-specific tax rates. These will override the global rate if
both are set.

## What happens if I set both a global and a country-specific tax rate?

The country-specific tax rate will take priority over the global rate.

## Do I need to configure anything in Shopify for this to work?

Yes, the countries you assign tax rates to must have markets created in your Shopify
admin.

## Where is the tax information shown to customers?

Once the country is selected on the frontend, customers will see both inclusive and
exclusive prices in the product page pricing grid.

## Is this tax setting applied during checkout?

No, the Tax Display feature is for display purposes only on the product pricing grid. The
actual tax charged at checkout depends on your Shopify tax settings.

## Can I apply tax display only for selected products?

Currently, tax display settings are applied globally across the storefront and not on a
per-product basis.
