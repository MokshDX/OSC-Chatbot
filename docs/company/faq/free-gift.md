# Free gift

*Advance FAQ*

## Will the free gift show as ₹0 / free in the cart?

Yes. The gift line shows the discount applied so the customer clearly sees it costs them
nothing.

## Can I give more than one free gift under the same rule?

Yes, a single rule can include more than one gift product.

## What can trigger a free gift?

You can base it on things like buying a specific product, buying a certain quantity, or
reaching a minimum cart total. You decide the condition when you create the rule.

## Can I choose who gets the free gift?

Yes. You can offer it to everyone, only to logged-in customers, or only to customers with
a specific tag (for example, your "wholesale" or "VIP" group).

## Do customers have to enter a code?

No. There's no coupon or discount code to type. As long as the cart meets the rule you set
up, the gift appears by itself.

## How does the customer get the gift?

Automatically. When their cart qualifies, the gift is added on its own. On the product
page they'll see a note like "Your free gifts will be added at checkout automatically," so
they know it's coming.

## What is the Free Gift feature?

It lets you automatically give customers a free product when they meet a condition you set
— like buying a certain item, reaching a quantity, or spending a minimum amount. The gift
is added to their order at checkout without them having to do anything.
