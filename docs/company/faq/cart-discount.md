# Cart Discount

*Advance FAQ*

## How do I test if the cart discount is working correctly?

Add products to your cart that meet the discount conditions and check on the cart page and
checkout page. You should see the discount automatically applied.

## Can I schedule cart discounts for specific dates or time periods?

This functionality is not available currently, but it's on our roadmap for future releases.

## Is it possible to apply cart discounts based on specific customer tags?

This is not supported by default. However, we are working on implementing customer tags
based discount logic in future updates.

## Can I create tiered discounts (e.g., buy more, save more)?

Yes. While creating a cart discount, you can define multiple tiers by entering different
minimum values and corresponding discount amounts.
