# Add-on Tier Pricing

*Advance FAQ*

## I only have one cost — a blank item cost. Can I still use tier pricing with a single margin?

Yes. The simplest setup uses just one cost parameter and one profit margin, applied across
all your quantity tiers. The result is a clean, flat wholesale price.

**Merchant benefit:** Great for resellers or distributors who only need a markup on a base
unit cost — no complexity, no extra steps.

**Example:** A "Navy Sports Jacket" priced at $3.20 cost + 20% margin = $3.84 per unit at
every tier.

## Can tier pricing handle a mixed cart of different products and multiple variants?

Yes. The app supports multiple products, each with multiple variants, all in the same
cart. Every variant uses its own quantity range to determine its tier price.

**Merchant benefit:** Ideal for wholesale buyers who place large multi-line orders —
pricing stays accurate without manual checks.

**Example:** A cart with 499 Ocean Blue Shirts (XS), 1 Ocean Blue Shirt (M), 53 Yellow
Wool Jumpers (S), and 3 Yellow Wool Jumpers (M) prices each line independently.

## If a customer orders multiple different products together, will each product use its own tier pricing?

Yes. Each product in the cart is evaluated on its own quantity, cost parameters, and
margins. The app keeps every line item priced correctly even when the cart contains
different products.

**Merchant benefit:** Buyers can mix and match without losing their bulk discount on any
individual product.

**Example:** A cart with 71 "Ocean Blue Shirts" and 11 "Yellow Wool Jumpers" applies the
50–71 tier to the shirts and the 6–11 tier to the jumpers — at the same time.

## Can the same tier pricing apply across all sizes of a product?

Absolutely. You can apply one tier structure to every variant of a product — XS, S, M, L,
and beyond — so every size benefits from the same volume discounts.

**Merchant benefit:** Set up your pricing once and let it scale across your full size
range. No duplicate work, no inconsistencies.

**Example:** A "Floral White Top" charges $21.89 per unit for 1–5 pieces in any size, and
$5.76 per unit at 500+ pieces — identical logic across XS, S, and M.

## Can I set quantity-based pricing for a single product with multiple cost components?

Yes. For a single product variant, you can define up to four cost parameters and two
profit margins across multiple quantity tiers. The app applies the correct price
automatically based on how many units the customer adds.

**Merchant benefit:** Perfect for branded items where each unit carries a base cost plus
setup, decoration, and shipping — and you want clean bulk pricing without manual work.

**Example:** A "Red Sports Tee" in size XS priced at $21.89 for 1–5 units drops to $5.76
each at 500+ units, with all four cost layers and both margins baked in.
