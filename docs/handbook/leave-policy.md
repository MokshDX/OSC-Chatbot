# Leave and Time Off Policy

Applies to all OSC employees on a permanent contract. Contractors are covered by
their individual statement of work.

## Annual leave

Permanent employees accrue **26 days** of paid annual leave per calendar year,
in addition to public holidays. Accrual begins on the first day of employment
and is credited monthly at 2.166 days per completed month.

A maximum of **10 unused days** may be carried into the following year. Carried
days expire on 31 March and are not paid out on expiry.

Leave requests of five consecutive days or fewer require approval from your
direct manager. Requests longer than five consecutive days additionally require
approval from the department head, and should be submitted at least 21 days in
advance.

## Sick leave

OSC provides **12 days** of fully paid sick leave per calendar year. Sick leave
does not accrue and unused days do not carry over.

A medical certificate is required for any absence exceeding **three consecutive
working days**. Absences of three days or fewer require only that you notify your
manager before 10:00 on the first day of absence.

Sick leave taken during a period of approved annual leave may be reclassified as
sick leave on production of a medical certificate.

## Parental leave

Primary caregivers are entitled to **18 weeks** of paid parental leave.
Secondary caregivers are entitled to **6 weeks** of paid parental leave. Both
must be taken within 12 months of the birth or placement date.

Parental leave may be taken in up to three separate blocks with manager
agreement. Notice of at least 8 weeks is required before the intended start date.

## Unpaid leave

Unpaid leave of up to three months may be granted at the discretion of the
department head, and requires that all accrued annual leave has been exhausted
first. Benefits continue during unpaid leave; pension contributions are suspended.

## Public holidays

OSC observes the public holidays of the employee's country of residence. Where an
employee works across regions, the holiday calendar of their contracting entity
applies.

## Sabbatical

After five years of continuous service employees may request a paid sabbatical of four weeks.
