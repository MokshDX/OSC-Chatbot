# Expense and Travel Policy

## Submission

All expenses must be submitted through the Expense Portal within **60 days** of
the date the cost was incurred. Claims submitted after 60 days require written
justification and finance director approval, and may be declined.

Every claim must carry an itemised receipt. Card statements are not accepted as
evidence. Expenses under 25 EUR may be claimed without a receipt, capped at
100 EUR per employee per month.

## Approval thresholds

| Amount | Approver |
|---|---|
| Up to 500 EUR | Direct manager |
| 500 to 2,500 EUR | Department head |
| Above 2,500 EUR | Finance director |

Approval must be obtained **before** the cost is incurred for any single item
above 2,500 EUR. Retrospective approval is not granted.

## Travel

Economy class is the standard for all flights under **six hours**. Premium
economy may be booked for flights of six hours or longer. Business class requires
finance director approval regardless of duration.

Accommodation is capped at **180 EUR per night** in tier-one cities (London,
Zurich, New York, Singapore, Tokyo) and **120 EUR per night** elsewhere. The cap
excludes local tourist taxes, which are reimbursable in full.

Rail travel should be preferred over air travel where the total door-to-door
journey time is under five hours.

## Meals

The daily meal allowance while travelling is **65 EUR** in tier-one cities and
**45 EUR** elsewhere. The allowance is a cap on reimbursable spend, not a per diem
payment: only actual documented spend is reimbursed.

Client entertainment requires prior approval from the department head and must
record the names and employers of all attendees.

## Non-reimbursable

The following are never reimbursed: traffic and parking fines, in-flight
purchases of duty-free goods, personal travel insurance, hotel minibar charges,
and loyalty-programme fees.
