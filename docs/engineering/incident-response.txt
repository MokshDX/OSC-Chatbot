OSC INCIDENT RESPONSE STANDARD
==============================

Scope: all production services owned by OSC engineering.

SEVERITY LEVELS
---------------

SEV1 - Complete loss of a customer-facing service, or confirmed data loss, or a
confirmed security breach. Response required within 15 minutes, 24 hours a day.
An incident commander must be appointed. Executive notification is mandatory.

SEV2 - Major functionality degraded for a significant number of customers, with
no workaround available. Response required within 30 minutes during business
hours, 1 hour outside them.

SEV3 - Minor functionality degraded, or a workaround exists. Response required
by the next business day.

SEV4 - Cosmetic issue or a single-customer report with no service impact.
Handled through the normal backlog.

ROLES
-----

Incident Commander owns the response and is the single decision maker. The IC
does not debug; the IC coordinates. For SEV1 the IC must not also be the primary
responder.

Communications Lead owns all internal and customer updates. Required for SEV1 and
SEV2.

Scribe records a timestamped log of actions taken and decisions made. Required
for SEV1.

COMMUNICATION CADENCE
---------------------

SEV1 - status update every 30 minutes until resolved, posted to the incident
channel and the customer status page.

SEV2 - status update every 2 hours during business hours.

SEV3 and SEV4 - update on resolution only.

POSTMORTEM
----------

A written postmortem is mandatory for every SEV1 and SEV2 incident and must be
published within 5 business days of resolution.

Postmortems are blameless. They describe what the system did and what conditions
allowed it, not who made a mistake. Every action item has a named owner and a due
date, and is tracked to completion in the normal engineering backlog.

A SEV1 postmortem is reviewed in the weekly engineering leadership meeting.
